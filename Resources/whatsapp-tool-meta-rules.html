part 3 — how we avoid getting our number banned ↓

Playing by Meta's rules

Part 1 had the ideas right. Here are the exact 2026 numbers — and the code that actually enforces each one.

1

Meta is watching 3 things

Break any of these and you get throttled, frozen, or banned. Everything in this doc protects one of them.

1 · Approval

Any message you start must use a template Meta pre-checked. No approval = it won't send.

2 · Quality

Blocks & "report spam" tank your quality rating (Green→Yellow→Red). Low quality = limits cut.

3 · Limits

You can only reach so many new people per 24h. Try more and the extras are rejected.

The golden rule

Only message opted-in people, relevant stuff, well-paced. Do that and limits rise on their own.

2

Rule A — Template approval

Every business-initiated message is a template in one of three buckets, and Meta reviews the wording (minutes to a day):

Marketing

Promos, offers, re-engagement. Pricier, and the most scrutinised.

Utility

Order updates, receipts, reminders. Cheap; free if sent inside the 24h window.

Authentication

OTPs / login codes. Strict format rules.

Gets rejected for: mismatched category, spammy wording, broken variables, or promo content hiding in a "utility" template. Meta can also re-categorise a template and bill it at the higher rate.

3

Rule B — Quality & delivery

Quality is a traffic light, judged on blocks, reports, and how people engage.

Green

Healthy. Limits keep climbing.

Yellow

Warning. Your tier freezes — no growth.

Red

Danger. Limit cut, or the number paused.

Two extra delivery rules that bite: a user can get only ~2 marketing messages per day across all brands (over that, Meta returns saturation error 131049 and drops it), and since April 2025 no marketing templates to US (+1) numbers at all.

4

Rule C — The sending limits

How many new people you can reach per rolling 24h. It's a ladder you climb by behaving.

The 2025–26 changes that trip people up: you must verify your business to move past 250 (no workaround); limits are now shared across every number in your Business Portfolio (since Oct 2025), so a new number inherits the portfolio's tier; and throughput is ~80 messages/sec by default (up to 1,000/sec at the top tier) — not the 40/sec figure in the old blueprint.

Exact intermediate thresholds shift over time — always read the limit from Meta's live business_capability_update webhook rather than hard-coding it.

6

Don't get caught out by these

Rules move

Limits, rates & categories change a few times a year. Read limits from the webhook; review Meta's docs quarterly. Don't hard-code numbers.

Webhook field change

The limit field renamed: max_daily_conversation_per_phone (v23 & older) is retiring; v24+ uses max_daily_conversations_per_business. Handle both through early 2026.

Verify first, build second

Until the business is verified, you're stuck at 250/day forever. Get verification started on day one — it takes a few days.

Warm up new numbers

Don't blast a fresh number. Start small, send relevant stuff, let the tier climb over days. Patience = higher ceilings.

obey these and the limits grow for free. fight them and the number dies. ✎
